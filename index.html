ReelForge

ReelForge is a personal video tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It edits short videos (trimming, vertical formatting, captions) and publishes them to the owner's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts using each platform's official API.

ReelForge is not a public service. It is used only by its owner (sirgeekski) to manage their own accounts. It does not offer sign-ups and does not access anyone else's accounts.
