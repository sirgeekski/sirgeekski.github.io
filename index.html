Turn long videos into short ones, and post them where you want.

ReelForge is a desktop app for creators. It cuts long videos into vertical Shorts, Reels and TikToks, adds captions and transitions, and publishes them to your own accounts through each platform's official API.

Features

✂ Clip long videos

Load a podcast, stream or vlog and ReelForge splits it into one-minute parts at sentence boundaries, or picks the best moments, ready to post as a numbered series.

🎬 Edit in vertical

Trim, crop to 9:16, add burned-in captions, colour grades, music and smooth transitions such as whip pans, zooms and blur dissolves.

💬 Automatic captions

Speech is transcribed on your own computer, so captions are word-accurate and your audio never leaves your machine for transcription.

📅 Scheduled publishing

Queue videos with a review step: you approve each one and its caption before it is posted, and posts are spaced out over the day.

📚 Media library

Keep source videos, finished exports and reusable editing templates in one place.

🔒 Runs locally

ReelForge runs on your own PC. Your videos, drafts and account connections are stored on your computer, not on our servers.

How it works

  1. Add a video. Import a file you own, or one you are licensed to use.
  2. Edit or clip it. Use the editor, or let ReelForge cut it into a series of parts.
  3. Review. Preview each clip and edit its title, caption and privacy setting.
  4. Publish. ReelForge uploads the clip to the account you connected, at the time you chose.

Supported platforms

TikTok

Connect with Login Kit and post with the Content Posting API. Before each post you see your TikTok username, choose who can view the video, and set comments, Duet and Stitch. You can also send videos to your TikTok inbox as drafts and finish them in the TikTok app.

YouTube

Upload Shorts with the YouTube Data API, with title, description, tags and privacy chosen by you.

Instagram

Publish Reels to an Instagram professional account with the Instagram Graph API, or download the clip and post it from the Instagram app.

You connect your accounts yourself through each platform's official sign-in page. ReelForge never sees your passwords, and you can disconnect at any time from the app or from the platform's settings.

FAQ

Who is ReelForge for?

Creators who repurpose their own long-form videos into short clips. It is currently used by its developer to manage their own channels.

Does ReelForge need an account?

No. There is no ReelForge account or login. You only sign in to the platforms you want to post to, on their own websites.

What data does ReelForge store?

Only what it needs to post for you, stored on your computer. See the privacy policy.

Does it post without my approval?

Not unless you turn the review step off. By default every clip waits for you to approve it and its caption.

How much does it cost?

ReelForge is free to use.
